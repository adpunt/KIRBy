   We compare Gaussian noise on the HOMO–LUMO gap to Laplace, Student-t and
outlier noise in Figure 3.6. The shape of the distribution from which the noise was
drawn made little difference. SVM reported higher AUCnorm values on non-Gaussian
label noise, 0.937 under Gaussian noise against 0.947, 0.952 and 0.955 under Student-
t, outlier and Laplace noise on PDV, while NGBoost lost AUCnorm under every one.
NGBoost’s relatively stronger performance on Gaussian makes sense as it fits a per-
molecule scale under a Gaussian likelihood, which the other distributions violate. SVM
gains AUCnorm and NGBoost loses it under all three distributions across all datasets and
representations. The size of the shift stays small, which is why these results coincide
