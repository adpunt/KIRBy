5.2.3    Augmentation
Some feature sets are too small or too specialized to take part in budget allocation, such
as MACCS fingerprints (167 features) or log-likelihood ratios (3 features). Others, such
as mutation features that record differences from a parent antibody, carry meaning
only beside a full representation. We therefore add them as augmentation, calling
the finished hybrid H the base and the feature sets that supply extra features the
donors. Donors are set per dataset (Augmentation column of Table 5.12). Uncapped
augmentation adds the extra features outside the budget, and capped augmentation
puts them in place of the weakest base features. An earlier search of each whole dataset
covered 16 datasets of an earlier list. On none did either augmented condition beat
no augmentation by more than 1% (3% on datasets under about 1,000 samples). We
therefore ran the searches in this chapter without augmentation on all 20 datasets
(Section 5.2.4). Only the nearest-neighbor search on SMAC colloidal stability and
HIC retention keeps uncapped augmentation (Chapter 6). Additional file 21 gives how
donor features are ranked and added, and the three conditions.


5.2.4    Search grid per dataset
Searching every value of Table 5.5 in every fold of every dataset was too slow, so
each dataset searches a smaller grid (Table 5.6). From an earlier search of each whole
dataset, we kept the two downstream models with the best scores. Likewise, we kept
the budgets whose ten best configurations scored within 1% of the best budget (3% on
datasets under about 1,000 samples), widened to at least three consecutive budgets.
Importance methods, allocation methods, internal evaluators and representation counts
that scored well below the best were dropped.
   Two datasets, QM9 and hERG blocking, were too slow for the earlier search over ev-
ery value. They were searched over a shorter fixed list, called the fast tier (LightGBM
gain importance; linear, squared and rank-decay allocation; three or all representa-
tions), and their grid stays within it. The search with the nearest-neighbor objective
has its own grid, given in Chapter 6.
