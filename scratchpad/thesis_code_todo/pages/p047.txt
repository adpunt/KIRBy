Robustness metrics

For regression, the package computes R2 , the root mean squared error (RMSE) and
the mean absolute error at every noise level. For classification, it computes accuracy,
together with macro- and weighted-averaged precision, recall and F1 score and a per-
class breakdown. Robustness is read from the retention curve ret(x) = m(x)/m(0),
where x is the noise level. Here m is a bounded metric for which higher values are better:
R2 for regression, and accuracy, F1, precision or recall for classification. The primary
summary is the normalized area under this curve, AUCnorm (Equation 3.2 in Chapter 3),
evaluated by the trapezoid rule. It is the mean fraction of clean performance retained
across the noise levels and lies approximately within [0, 1]. It is also less directly
correlated with clean-label R2 than other robustness metrics we tested (Chapter 3). A
secondary summary fits ret(x) ≈ exp −(x/τW )β to the curve. The shape parameter
                                                

β separates curves that hold and then collapse (β > 1) from curves that fall early
and then plateau (β < 1). The parameter τW sets the scale of the decay, and the fit
requires at least four noise levels. The package also reports the clean baseline and the
retention at the highest noise level, flags any curve that falls below zero retention, and
can withhold the curve summaries for models whose clean baseline is too low.
