choice of model family, while the replicate and residual take more. This means that the
variance is left more up to chance and to the underlying variation within the data itself
with label noise, which is the expected behavior. Robustness divides the variance a bit
differently. Representation explains 8.1% of the variance in AUCnorm , against 31.1%
of clean R2 , while model family rises to 47.2%. The replicate nearly disappears from
AUCnorm , likely because each replicate’s curve is divided by that replicate’s own clean
R2 , so a difficult split lowers both and cancels out.
   We include two terms which stem from the data rather than the model, the residual
and replicate terms. The replicate measures how much of the variance can be attributed
to the train and test split, which in this case is a scaffold split. On QM9 we replicated
each experiment 10 times with different random draws of N = 10,000, each time giving
a new scaffold split. On the assay datasets, a replicate refers to one of the five scaffold
CV folds. A large replicate term in ANOVA indicates that the folds differ from one
another. The residual is variance that no other term accounts for.
   While we observe similar patterns between noise conditions, the predictive per-
formance of grouped-shifted noise in particular varies significantly between replicates,
resulting in a larger share for the replicate. We find that 28.4% of the variance in
AUCnorm and 51.0% of the variance in R2 are due to the replicate, with model family
falling to 32.7% and 8.4%. This indicates that the offset applied to each scaffold may
account for more of the variance in both R2 and AUCnorm .
