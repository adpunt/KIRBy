Statement on the Use of Generative
AI

I declare that my use of generative AI in this thesis complies with the University
of Oxford’s policy on generative AI in research and summative assessment. It also
complies with the guidance of the Mathematical, Physical and Life Sciences Division
for postgraduate research students and of the Department of Statistics.
   The research questions, methods, experimental design, interpretation of results and
conclusions are my own, except where a citation or the Statement of Contribution says
otherwise. All written text in this thesis is my own.
   I used Claude (Anthropic), through the Claude web application and Claude Code,
from February 2026 to October 2026. The models were Claude Opus 4.5, 4.6, 4.7, 4.8,
5 and 5.5, Claude Sonnet 5 and 5.5, and Claude Fable 5 and 5.1. Before that, I used
ChatGPT (OpenAI), with GPT-5.1 and GPT-5.2, from December 2025 to January
2026.
   I used Claude and ChatGPT to discuss research ideas I had already formed, for
example by asking how a proposed method might fail or what related methods existed.
The contributions of this thesis are my own. They include the adaptive hybrid search
and the design of its search space (Chapter 5), and the cliff-aware gap, a score for finding
noisy labels (Chapter 7). I did not adopt any AI output as a research contribution.
   I wrote the original Python and Rust data pipelines by hand. I then used Claude,
mostly through Claude Code, to extend them and to implement methods from my
own specifications, including the adaptive hybrid search and the noise mitigation ex-
periments. I also used it to write unit tests and to debug. I reviewed and tested all
AI-assisted code, and checked it against the methods described in this thesis. I take full
responsibility for the correctness of all code. Before February 2026 I used ChatGPT
for the same purposes.
