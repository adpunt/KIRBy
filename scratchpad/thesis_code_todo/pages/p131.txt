an extreme descriptor value could make a fold’s error several orders of magnitude larger
on rat microsomal clearance. The experiments where this happened have no result for
that dataset until they are re-run. The Gaussian process now starts its kernel length
scale at the square root of the number of features rather than at 1. On inputs of over
1,000 features, a start of 1 left some fits predicting one value for every sample. Most
DNN results in this chapter were computed before the first correction, and the hybrids
of the four Gaussian-process datasets were chosen by searches run before the second.
We report them as computed and name the correction beside each result it affects.
    Summed over its 5 outer folds, the search of one dataset took 2.3 hours (hERG Ki )
to 28.2 hours (MDR1–MDCK efflux) on small molecules, and 11.1 hours on Buchwald–
Hartwig yield. On antibodies it took 0.4 hours (CHO polyreactivity) to 38.6 hours (the
anti-VEGF expression scan). The hERG Ki total is likely too low, since one of its folds
recorded only 90 seconds. Each fold ran as its own job on a CPU node of the Oxford
ARC or HTC cluster, with 8 cores and 64 GB of memory by default. The times count
only each fold’s last attempt, which undercounts a fold resumed from a stopped run.


5.2.9     Comparators, controls and ablations
The hybrid is the best of many configurations; hence its gain may only reward a
large search. Each experiment below asks one question about this or a related issue.
Table 5.13 lists them, with the settings each varies and holds fixed.
    Unless stated otherwise, every experiment below uses the dataset’s grid (Table 5.6),
its two downstream models, five outer folds and the dataset’s own metric. From each
fold’s search it takes, for each downstream model, the two single representations and
the two hybrids with the best scores on the training samples.


Best single representation and PCA

As a second reference beside the best single representation, every base representation is
joined, standardized on the training samples and reduced by PCA [34] to each budget
of the dataset’s grid. The downstream model is fitted on the principal components as
they are. On each fold, the hybrid is compared with PCA at the hybrid’s own budget;
in this way, neither side’s width is chosen on the held-out samples. PCA cannot have
more components than training samples, and where the budget is larger the two widths
differ.
