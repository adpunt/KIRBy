5.2.10      Transfer, collaboration studies and separate sites
The first study asks whether search settings found on a large dataset can be reused on a
small one instead of a new search. The settings are the importance method, allocation
method, budget B and internal evaluator. The source is LogD, with 5,028 molecules,
and the target is hERG pKi , with 1,412 molecules. Both use the seven representations
the two datasets share: Avalon, ChemBERT, ECFP4, GROVER, MHG-GNN, Mordred
and SMI-TED. The source settings come from one search on every LogD molecule, none
of which is in the target. The closest prior work, Meta-QSAR [242], chose the learning
method and representation for a new QSAR dataset from results on over 2,700 others
and, like this study, reused decisions rather than selected features. We have not found a
paper that applies feature indices selected on one QSAR dataset unchanged to another,
although our literature search was not complete.
