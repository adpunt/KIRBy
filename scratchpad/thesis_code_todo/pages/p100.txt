4.4     Conclusions
On a single dataset, often very little of a standard representation varies. On hERG Ki ,
1,815 of the 2,048 ECFP4 features take one value in at least 95% of the 1,412 molecules
(Section 4.3.1). In addition, several representations give different molecules, mostly
stereoisomers, the same feature vector, and no model can separate those molecules,
whatever their labels. ECFP4 gives 56 of the 1,412 hERG Ki molecules a vector
shared with another molecule, all of them stereoisomers (“Molecules with identical
feature vectors”).
   A distance sums over every feature, so when only a few features matter for the
property, the rest dominate the sum (Section 4.3.2). Across 70 combinations of dataset
and representation, the Spearman correlation between pair distance and label difference
was below 0.10 in 44. Predicting each label from its five nearest neighbors gave a higher
Spearman correlation in all 70, so the useful information sits in a few close pairs rather
than in the distance as a whole.
   We found that methods built on a distance did worst under label noise. In the
small sweep of published noise-handling methods, the configurations with the lowest
retention all used a distance (Section 4.3.3), and judging a label by its neighbors’ labels
usually made the model worse (Section 4.3.3).
