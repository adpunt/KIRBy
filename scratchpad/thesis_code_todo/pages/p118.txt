5.2.5       Evaluation protocol
Every experiment splits each dataset into outer folds as set out in Section 5.2.6. The
search sees only the training samples of each outer fold, and the selected features are
rebuilt on them and applied unchanged to the held-out samples. Only three experi-
ments limit each held-out feature to the range it takes on the training samples: the
comparison with the best single representation, the easy and hard halves, and the
comparison of many and few representations.
   Because of time and computational constraints, not every experiment in Chapters 5
to 7 ran on every fold or every dataset. Some experiments here ran on the first three
outer folds, most noise experiments in Chapter 7 ran on the first outer fold only, and
some runs had not finished when this thesis was written. We report the results that
exist, give the number of folds beside each one, and name the datasets where a result
is missing. We do not repeat this caveat at every result.
   The hybrid is compared with the best single base representation at its full number
of features, which is what a user would otherwise run. This comparator is chosen per
downstream model and per fold from the search’s scores on the outer training samples,
and it can differ between folds. It is then refit on those samples and scored on the same
held-out samples as the hybrid. The hybrid is held to its budget, and the comparator
is consequently often many times wider. Both widths are recorded for every fold, since
a difference between the two scores is partly a difference in width. The main quantity
