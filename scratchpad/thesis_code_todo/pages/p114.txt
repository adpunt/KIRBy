   The first method of feature allocation we tried was a greedy forward selection, in
which the hybrid is built one block of features at a time [33, 176]. In each round,
the next block is added from representations in order of which improves the internal
validation score. This in theory lets it choose features for how well they work together.
In practice, this is very expensive. With blocks of 10 features and a budget of 500 and
10 representations, it needs up to 500 model fits per configuration and fold against
10 for a score-based method. It was also less accurate: in preliminary runs on Jain
antibody neonatal Fc receptor (FcRn) retention it placed 16th of 17 allocation methods,
with a Spearman correlation of 0.360 against 0.453 for linear allocation. We therefore
removed it from the search (Additional file 16).
