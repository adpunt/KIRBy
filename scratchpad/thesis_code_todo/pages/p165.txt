   • The four Ginkgo developability datasets in this chapter (AC-SINS self-
      association, SMAC colloidal stability, CHO polyreactivity and expression titer)
      use the competition’s five folds as their five groups. Inside an outer fold, each
      held-out block of the search is then a single group, so no antibody has a neighbor
      outside its own group.

   • On the change in binding free energy (∆∆G) caused by a mutation, one protein
      complex holds 69 of the 429 rows. On outer folds 1 to 4 it falls alone into one
      held-out block of the search, and every candidate is refused. We skip this dataset
      on all five folds.
