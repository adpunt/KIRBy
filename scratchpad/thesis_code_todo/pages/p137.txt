   Two further studies come from a collaboration with the Oxford Protein Informatics
Group. The first repeats the activity-cliff QSAR setting of Dablander et al. [57] on
three targets, with five scaffold folds. Dopamine D2 (6,333 compounds) and factor Xa
(3,605) come from ChEMBL, and the SARS-CoV-2 main protease (1,924) comes from
the COVID Moonshot project. Each target’s pool should hold 10 representations, but
GROVER and SMI-TED were not built, leaving eight for the search. The downstream
models are LightGBM and the DNN, and the metric is the mean absolute error (MAE)
in log units of activity.
   The second study counts the molecules that a representation cannot tell apart,
meaning that two or more molecules get an identical feature row, on every represen-
tation of each dataset (Chapter 4), and asks whether a hybrid removes the problem.
The representation with the most such molecules on hERG Ki , the WL graph kernel,
is the target. Two hybrids add features of two partner representations to it. One adds
ECFP4 and PDV, which are also blind to stereochemistry. The other adds ECFP4 and
MHG-GNN, which give no two molecules the same row. We report the score of each
hybrid against the graph kernel alone, and the number of molecules that still share a
row in the hybrid.
   Federated learning trains one model across data held by several parties without
moving the data [243]. It has been run across pharmaceutical companies [244], whose
structures and assay results are commercial assets that cannot be pooled [18, 19]. Our
collaborator, Lhasa Limited, uses the teacher-student process of Chapter 2 (Table 2.1)
[149]. We use their code for the fingerprints, site models and site clustering, and
re-implement from it the step that combines the site models’ predictions.
   Our addition is that feature selection, as well as model fitting, takes place at the
sites. One search on the training molecules of all sites together chooses the settings
(allocation method, importance method and internal evaluator). Each site then selects
features with these settings on its own molecules and reports only feature indices,
importance values and one score. A coordinator splits the total budget B across sites
in proportion to their scores, with at least one feature for each site whose selection
