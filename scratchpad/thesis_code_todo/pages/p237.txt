   The features matter as well: when the noise methods failed, we followed the failure
down to the distance between molecules and then to the features that distance is com-
puted over. The adaptive hybrid representation selects those features for each dataset
and model. On small molecules it predicted better than any single representation and
gave better neighbors. It stayed ahead, moreover, through every noise treatment.
   A new foundation model appears every few months in molecular and protein model-
ing, antibodies included. With the hybrid search, a researcher does not need to choose
between their current representation and a new one. They add the new embedding to
the search and rerun it, and the search decides whether the embedding contributes fea-
tures and which (tested on LogD and hERG blocking in Chapter 5). A choice usually
made by habit becomes one measured on each task.
