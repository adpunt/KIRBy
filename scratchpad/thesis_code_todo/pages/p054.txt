scaled by 1/N [120]. Because only its output layer keeps a learned observation-noise
parameter, VBLL networks report only epistemic uncertainty per molecule, with one
aleatoric variance for the whole fit. The fourth makes observation noise a function of
the input. All eight probabilistic networks estimate their predictive distributions from
100 stochastic forward passes. Each network and its Bayesian counterpart were tuned
to the same set of parameters so that NN-α against BNN-α and NN-β against BNN-β
differ by the Bayesian layers alone.
   The thirteen base models are RF, QRF, XGBoost, LightGBM, NGBoost, SVM,
the RBF-kernel GP, and NN, BNN and VBLL in each of the two architectures. The
six variant models are RF (300 trees); GP (het.), the heteroscedastic GP; BNN-α (var.
head) and BNN-β (var. head), the two Bayesian networks with a variance head; and
VBLL-α (het.) and VBLL-β (het.), the two heteroscedastic VBLL networks.


3.2.4     Noise conditions and levels
The seven noise conditions are defined in Section 2.5.1.
   We controlled noise quantity using noise levels, representing a fraction of the stan-
dard deviation of clean training labels. We ran experiments across seven levels, 0, 0.2,
0.3, 0.5, 0.75, 1.0 and 1.5. We added the noise to the raw label and standardized the
target afterwards, using the clean training mean and spread. To control the quantity of
noise for censoring, we recorded every label above the kth largest clean training label
as that limit and used this level as the fraction clipped, at 0, 0.1, 0.2, 0.25, 0.3, 0.4 and
0.5. Censoring is common in pharmaceutical assays, where a third or more of labels
can be censored [83].
   We ran Gaussian, grouped-wider and grouped-shifted noise on every model and
representation pairing. The remaining four conditions ran on selected pairs.
   On QM9, Laplace, Student-t and outlier noise were run on eight models, namely RF,
NGBoost, SVM, the GP, GP (het.), BNN-α (var. head), VBLL-α (het.) and VBLL-β
(het.), each on ECFP4, PDV and ChemBERTa. Censoring was run on five pairings,
namely RF and GP (het.) on ECFP4, and NGBoost, the GP and BNN-α (var. head)
on PDV. On the three assay datasets, AUCnorm under censoring was computed for the
same five pairings, and the uncertainty statistics under censoring for QRF and the
other models that give an uncertainty estimate, on ECFP4, PDV and ChemBERTa.
