6.2     Methods
Datasets, folds (grouped by scaffold family for small molecules) and metrics are as in
Chapter 5, with five outer folds. Some experiments have no result on some datasets
because their runs did not finish in time (Section 5.2.5), and Section 6.4 lists them.
MDR1–MDCK efflux was added late and has no row in the tables of distance in the
predictive hybrids and in each feature space, the best number of features, importance
weighting in the four models, and activity cliffs. In each outer fold, everything an ex-
periment compares is chosen from searches on that fold’s training molecules alone, and
then scored on its held-out molecules. Features are scaled on the training molecules:
features that only take the values 0 and 1 are left as they are, and the rest are stan-
dardized. In the experiments, but not inside the searches, held-out values are cut to
the training range as well, which stops a single extreme molecule from dominating
a distance. Where a statement covers all 13 datasets, “molecule” also stands for an
antibody or a reaction.
   The fold split, the sample of molecule pairs, the label permutations, the random
feature subsets and the k-means starts all use fixed seeds. The spread we report is
therefore the spread across the five outer folds of one split. We report the mean over
the five folds, and the per-fold values where the spread matters, without significance
tests. The simulation of choosing molecules to measure is repeated over three starting
sets instead.


6.2.1     Feature spaces compared
Each outer fold runs three searches on its training molecules. The first, the general
search, is the search of Chapter 5, run for each of the dataset’s two downstream models
(Table 5.6). The second, the distance search, uses a k-nearest-neighbor model as its
downstream model, with Euclidean distance and five equally weighted neighbors, or
fewer when a fold has under 25 training molecules. It has its own lists of importance
methods, allocation methods, numbers of representations and budgets. Its budgets are
capped at 500 features, and on some datasets its other lists differ from the general
search’s too. On hERG Ki , for example, the general search tries 250, 500, 1,000 and
