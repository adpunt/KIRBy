Statement of Contribution

Chapter 3 is adapted from a jointly authored manuscript, Punt, A., Hanser, T., Werner,
S., and Morris, G.M., NoiseInject: A Benchmarking Framework to Study Noise Robust-
ness in QSAR Models, which will be released as a preprint and submitted to the Journal
of Cheminformatics. Most of the figures, tables and text in that chapter are identical or
similar to the manuscript. I implemented the NoiseInject framework, conducted all ex-
periments, and drafted the manuscript. T.H., S.W., and G.M. supervised the project,
contributed to the experimental design, and revised the manuscript. NoiseInject is
available at https://github.com/adpunt/noise_inject.

Chapters 5 to 7 describe the adaptive hybrid representation and its use under label
noise. We intend to submit this work as two manuscripts with G.M., S.W., and T.H.
as co-authors: one on the adaptive hybrid representation (Chapters 5 and 6) and one
on detecting and handling label noise (Chapter 7). I conceived the adaptive hybrid
search and designed the system, conducted all experiments, and wrote these chapters.
The code was implemented with Claude (Anthropic) within that design, as described
in the Statement on the Use of Generative AI. G.M., S.W., and T.H. supervised the
work. The code for the adaptive hybrid search is available at https://github.com/
adpunt/KIRBy.
