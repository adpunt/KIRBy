6.3.4    Prediction error, retrieval and clustering
A molecule’s distance to its nearest training molecules rises weakly with the error made
on it (Table 6.14). The distance hybrid tracks error better for both models on QM9,
Caco-2, hERG Ki and LogD, and worse for both on hERG blocking. The largest change
is on hERG Ki , from 0.200 to 0.276 Spearman with the support vector machine’s errors,
while on LogD it is small.
   On nanobody thermostability, ∆∆G, expression titer, CHO polyreactivity and AC-
SINS self-association, the hybrid tracks error better for both models (Table 6.14).
It tracks error worse for both on SMAC colloidal stability, where the network goes
from 0.136 to 0.030 Spearman, and on Buchwald–Hartwig. Distance in the single
representation does not rise with error for either model on Buchwald–Hartwig, ∆∆G
and expression titer.
   With the setting picked again for each fold, the hybrid retrieves neighbors with
closer labels on all six small-molecule datasets, and QM9’s label gap falls from 0.601
to 0.393 (Table 6.15). On hERG blocking, LogD, MDR1–MDCK efflux and QM9, the
hybrid’s k-means clusters are also more coherent at 3, 5 and 10 clusters (Table 6.16).
