means more than twice the median absolute deviation of the labels. On datasets whose
label is not a biological activity, such as LogD, the same test finds pairs that differ
sharply in that property, and we keep the name. The cliff set is rebuilt in every fold
from the damaged training labels. Its control puts back the same number of molecules
drawn at random from the non-cliff molecules. We found no earlier method that puts
examples back after a cleanup. Some cleaning methods exempt a whole class decided
in advance [271, 272], and Brodley and Friedl [155] name the risk of discarding correct
exceptions without giving a rule.
   The hybrid’s features are chosen against the labels, which means the labels can
be cleaned before the features are chosen or after. One experiment compares the two
orders, with nine cleanups that use the out-of-fold forest residual as the detector, under
outlier noise and on labels as delivered. The search and the fit each use the labels as
given or the cleaned labels, four combinations in all. Down-weighting cannot reach
the search, because no weight enters the feature ranking or the budget split. Its two
cleaned-search arms consequently do not exist. No source we found crosses features
chosen on cleaned or recorded labels with a fit on cleaned or recorded labels. The
nearest design ranks features on one version of the data and fits on another, but its
second step is class-imbalance sampling, not label cleaning [273].
   On labels as delivered, nothing says which molecules are wrong. A gain can come
from removing hard molecules rather than wrong ones. One experiment runs seven
cleanups on delivered labels with the neighbor gap and the forest residual as detec-
tors. Its control shuffles each detector’s scores across the training molecules, at three
seeds, and reruns the cleanup with the same share and strength. The same number of
molecules is touched, but not the ones the detector picked.
