   Distance-based reasoning relies on the assumption that the data embeddings con-
tain quality information. When the labels are corrupted or the important features are
scarce, similarity scores over-rely on compounding false or useless information, and the
assumption that similar molecules have similar properties breaks down. Bajusz et al.
[75] found that the Tanimoto, Dice and Cosine coefficients and the Soergel distance
rank molecules almost identically on binary fingerprints. We take this to mean that
changing the formula cannot compensate for a weak representation. This problem is
