five-neighbor prediction uses only the closest pairs.

Agreement with shared structure

For 500 random pairs per dataset we found the maximum common substructure (MCS)
with RDKit [32], allowing 2 seconds per pair and leaving out pairs that ran out of time.
The MCS distance is 1 − n/(a + b − n), where n is the number of atoms in the common
substructure and a and b are the numbers of heavy atoms in the two molecules. Its
Spearman correlation with ECFP4 Tanimoto distance ranged from 0.20 (hERG Ki and
HLM clearance) to 0.51 (Caco-2 and LogD).
