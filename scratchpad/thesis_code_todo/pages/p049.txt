3.1      Introduction
In this chapter, we inject the noise conditions of Chapter 2 into training labels and
measure how much predictive performance each pairing of representation and model
keeps.
   While there have been many investigations into the effect of noise on molecular
property prediction, direct comparisons between how different molecular representa-
tions respond to label noise remain limited. The choice of molecular representation
certainly affects predictive performance. Deng et al. [108] evaluated 62,820 models and
found that fixed representations, in most datasets RDKit 2D descriptors, outperformed
representations learned from SMILES and molecular graphs. Whether these conclu-
sions translate to noise robustness is our question. Between different types of label
