3.3.6    Uncertainty under increasing label noise
Until now we’ve discussed how much predictive capabilities a model is able to retain
while its training labels are corrupted with noise. Now, we consider how confident
the model reports its own predictions are in the presence of noise, and whether or not
those estimates match the increased label noise. Kolmar and Grulke [10] found that
the mean predicted uncertainty derived from the GPs increases with the amount of
