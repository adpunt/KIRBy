7.3.4     Detection of the suspect labels
This section uses injected damage alone (Section 7.2.2). The out-of-fold forest residual
finds outliers, shifted labels and flipped classes well above chance on every dataset it ran
on (Table 7.15). On Caco-2 under outlier noise its average precision is 0.338, against
a chance level of 0.051. Censoring is the exception on small molecules and reactions.
At a clipped share of 0.25 the residual falls below chance on Buchwald–Hartwig, LogD
and QM9, and is only a little above it on Caco-2 and hERG Ki . On SMAC colloidal
stability it stays well above chance. A model fitted to clipped labels predicts values
near the limit for the clipped molecules, which leaves their residuals small. Chapter 3
found the same in the models’ own uncertainty, which was lowest on the clipped labels.
   The cliff-aware gap does better than the residual when only some labels are shifted.
Under the shift in 10%, its median neighbor setting reaches 0.269 on Caco-2 against
0.202 for the residual. Under outliers its best setting is ahead and its median setting
behind, and under censoring it trails the residual on Caco-2, hERG Ki and LogD at
every setting (Table 7.15). No detector is best everywhere: a neighbor detector is
better at a shift that moves a few labels a long way, and a model residual is as good
or better at outliers.
