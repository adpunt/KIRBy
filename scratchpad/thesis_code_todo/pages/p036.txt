2.3.4    Bayesian neural networks
Standard feed-forward networks are deterministic; however, they can be transformed
into probabilistic models (Figure 2.1).
   Unlike standard deterministic ML methods, Bayesian methods model distribu-
tions over predictions using Bayes’ rule, allowing for the quantification of uncertainty
within parameters and labels. Bayesian methods treat the model parameters as ran-
dom variables with distributions. Uncertainty can also be estimated from other non-
deterministic methods, including deep ensembles [71] and bootstrap modeling [72].


2.4     Molecular similarity and activity cliffs
Much of cheminformatics rests on the similar property principle: structurally similar
molecules tend to have similar properties, including biological activity [58, 73]. Nearest-
neighbor prediction and GP kernels rely on it, and so do many noise detection methods.
Yet similarity is defined only relative to a representation and a coefficient [74]. Two
molecules that are near-identical under a substructure fingerprint may be far apart
under a PDV. A computed similarity value is easily over-interpreted [74].
