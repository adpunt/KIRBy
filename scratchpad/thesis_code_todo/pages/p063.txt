performance drops sharply after noise level 1.0.
   As we’ve seen, the choice of representation can have an impact on noise robustness,
but that impact is determined by model family, represented by the interaction term
between model and representation in Figure 3.5. Some models are more impacted
by representation than others, though. In general, networks span a wider range of
AUCnorm across the six representations than any of the other models. Between ECFP4
and PDV under Gaussian noise, the four plain and Bayesian networks differ by between
0.037 and 0.065 of AUCnorm , while no tree or kernel model shifts by more than 0.011.
VBLLs are an exception, as no two variational networks differ by more than 0.019.
