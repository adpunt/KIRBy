second run about half beat the plain network, within the network’s own spread. Where
one of them helps a measured dataset as delivered, the gain is therefore more likely
noise found. The heteroscedastic GP on ∆∆G is the clearest case: it raises the best

   Noise is removed most clearly, then, where deletion or a published method helps an
antibody dataset as delivered and does nothing for QM9. On the small-molecule labels
as delivered only label smoothing on hERG Ki clears the bar. The test rests on one
computed property of one dataset, on the first outer fold, and several methods have no
QM9 result yet (Section 7.4).


7.3.3    Changing a label against deleting a molecule
Under hERG’s label flips every action helped, and deleting the flagged molecules helped
most (Table 7.6). This holds for gradient-boosted trees on both the hybrid and the best
single representation, with 10% of labels flipped along a confusion matrix. Deletion is
the one action here with a control, and it passes it. With the neighbor-label gap as the
detector, deleting takes the hybrid to 0.838 AUROC against 0.818 for the best of five
random deletions of the same size. With the forest residual as the detector, deletion
beats the best random deletion on all three folds that have results (Table 7.7).
