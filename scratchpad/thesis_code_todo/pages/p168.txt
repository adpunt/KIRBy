Importance weighting in four models

We score every feature space with and without importance weighting, using four models
that read a distance. The first three are prediction from nearest neighbors at every
setting, a Gaussian process with an RBF kernel and a support vector regressor with
an RBF kernel. The fourth is each downstream model given four extra features from
its nearest training molecules, following q-RASAR [250]. The four extra features are
the neighbors’ mean label and its spread, the distance to the nearest neighbor and the
mean distance to the neighbors. They are computed at the three neighbor settings
that rank best in a screen on the training molecules. The Gaussian process and the
support vector regressor run on regression data only. A separate experiment applies the
weighting to all representations joined, on regression datasets, once for each importance
method.


Activity cliffs

An activity cliff is a pair of structurally similar molecules with very different activity
[58, 77]. Only the two hERG datasets measure an activity, but we keep the term for the
other three small-molecule datasets. We decide which molecules are cliff molecules in
