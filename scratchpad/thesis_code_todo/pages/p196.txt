   hERG IC50 carries its own known flag: how many times each molecule was mea-
sured, and how far apart those measurements are. A molecule is one canonical SMILES
string as given by ChEMBL. Its count is its number of ChEMBL IC50 records, and its
spread is the variance of their pIC50 values. Of the three methods that use the flag,
the first weights each molecule by its count. The second weights it by the inverse of
its estimated error variance, 1/(σu2 + s̃2i /ni ) [278]. Here ni is the number of measure-
ments behind the label, and σu2 is the error that remains however often a molecule is
measured. Its spread s̃2i is pulled toward the spread pooled over all molecules, as if the
pool were three more measurements. We do this because a variance from two or three
records is unreliable [279]. The third moves each label toward an out-of-fold LightGBM
prediction, further for a less precise label. The share moved is vi /(vi + τ 2 ), where vi is
the molecule’s spread over its count and τ 2 is the variance of the out-of-fold residual.
Held-out molecules are graded only if they have three or more measurements.


Every molecule

One method moves every label, with no selection at all. Each label moves halfway
toward the out-of-fold prediction of a LightGBM model fitted with a Huber loss [98],
whose threshold is 1.4826 times the median absolute out-of-fold residual. The Huber
loss keeps the outlying labels from pulling that prediction.


7.2.4     Noise-robust training
Two experiments test noise-robust training (Table 2.3): one changes the downstream
model, and one reruns the twelve published methods.


Changing the model instead of the labels

A model can be fitted more carefully to noisy labels with no label changed, and that is
the comparison point for every mitigation result. One experiment changes the down-
stream model in four ways. Retuning searches 20 random settings of the model plus
its default, scores each on three inner folds split by scaffold family of the damaged
training labels, and refits the best. Its control runs the same search on the clean labels
and fits the chosen setting to the damaged ones. No real user has that control, but
it tells us whether a gain comes from tuning against the noise or from tuning at all.
