Distance against label difference

For one feature space, this experiment estimates the Spearman correlation between the
distance of two held-out molecules and the absolute difference in their labels. A positive
value means that molecules further apart differ more in their labels. We estimate it in
each outer fold from a sample of up to 50,000 pairs. We measure it first in each of the
dataset’s representations alone, under every distance of Table 6.1, with no selection and
no importance weighting. We then measure it in the feature spaces of Section 6.2.1,
unweighted and weighted.
