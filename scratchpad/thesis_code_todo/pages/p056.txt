fit. We sought to determine if, as expected, the aleatoric component rises with injected
label noise while the epistemic component does not.
   For the BNN and VBLL transformations, we took the two components over T = 100
stochastic forward passes [119]:

                                  T                              T
                              1X                              1X                   2
               û2ale (x) =         v̂t (x),   û2epi (x) =         µ̂t (x) − µ̄(x) ,   (3.1)

where µ̂t (x) and v̂t (x) are the mean and the variance returned for molecule x on pass
t, and µ̄(x) is the mean over passes. Here v̂t (x) is the predicted observation noise
variance. It varies per molecule in the variance-head and heteroscedastic variants, and
is one number for the whole fit in the VBLL transformation. A full-BNN without a
variance head returns no variance, so its uncertainty is epistemic alone.
   The other models follow the same split. For GPs, the epistemic component is the
posterior variance of the latent function and the aleatoric component is the learned
observation noise [115]. All variants of the GP produce one number for the whole fit
except heteroscedastic. For QRF models, each tree holds multiple training molecules.
So for a given molecule, the aleatoric component is the variance of the labels in the
molecule’s given leaf. The total aleatoric uncertainty is those variances averaged over
the trees. The epistemic component is the variance of the leaf means across the trees.
NGBoost predicts one variance for each molecule from a single fit, which is aleatoric
alone [63]. In every model, the total uncertainty is the sum of the aleatoric and epis-
temic components.
   No held-out label carries injected noise, so every comparison against the injected
noise was made on training molecules. Each training set was divided into five folds
grouped on Bemis–Murcko scaffolds, and each molecule was scored by a refit that
excluded its own fold. For NGBoost on QM9, three of the five folds were scored to
limit its cost. No uncertainty was calibrated after fitting.
