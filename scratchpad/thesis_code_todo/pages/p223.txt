Small molecules and the reaction set

On the small-molecule labels as delivered, only hERG Ki ’s label smoothing gain is both
larger than the network’s own spread and passes its control (Section 7.3.3). Buchwald–
Hartwig is the one dataset where deleting flagged reactions from the labels as delivered
beats random deletion.


Repeat measurements on hERG IC50

hERG IC50 is the one dataset whose noise was measured rather than injected. Weight-
ing each molecule by its number of measurements took the support vector regressor on
the hybrid from 0.760 to 0.728 RMSE on the first draw, and helped on all three draws
(Table 7.26). Weighting by the inverse of the estimated error variance also helped it,
by less. The same count weights made the network worse. For the network, adding
the log of the measurement count as a feature helped instead, and so did shrinking
each label toward a prediction by its spread. NGBoost gained a little from the same
extra feature, and the interval model did slightly worse than the point model. Training
on one row per measurement instead of the mean barely changed the score for either
model. The detection of molecules flagged by ChEMBL curators has not run.
