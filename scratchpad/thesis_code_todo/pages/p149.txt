   Across the configurations of a search, the choice of downstream model accounts
for more of the variation in score than the budget on most datasets with a result
(Table 5.20). On LogD, for example, it accounts for about half of the variance and
the budget for almost none. The budget still interacts with the model’s own settings
(Additional file 29). With the SVM strongly regularized (C of 0.01 or 0.1), the search
picks the same budget on every fold of LogD, rat microsomal clearance, MDR1–MDCK
efflux and Caco-2. With C = 10 it picks larger budgets on LogD and rat microsomal
clearance, since a model that is allowed to fit more can use more features. The width
of the DNN does not move its pick in the same way: on LogD it takes the top of the
grid at every width, on all folds but one.
