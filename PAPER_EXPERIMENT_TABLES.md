# Experiment tables

Four tables: complexity, similarity, noise, OPIG. One row per experiment.

Coverage is counted from the result jsons, not from `analysis/*.md` — those dumps were
written 2026-10-08, are truncated at 400 lines per dataset, and the result folders have
been written to since. Where a row says a dump was unusable, that is the dump and not the
json; the jsons under `/Volumes/seagate/kirby_results_2026-10-10b/` are complete.
Patient data is excluded from every count. Full numbers and the unreadable-result notes
for each row are in `PAPER_OUTLINE_NOTES.md`.

Rows read directly from the jsons so far: c01, c02 (2026-10-29).

## Noise

Two things hold across almost the whole suite, so they are not repeated per row. **Only
outer fold 0 of 5 ran** in n02, n06, n07, n09, n11, n12, n13, n17, n18, n19, n20 and n21;
n01, n14 and n16 ran all five and n15 ran three to five. And **every stored result predates
the 2026-10-26 and 2026-10-29 code fixes** — files are dated 2026-10-07 to 2026-10-10,
and the five fixing commits all landed after.

## OPIG

Fifteen entries: fourteen experiment files plus one support directory. Six have scores,
nine do not. Two faults touch almost every OPIG number that exists: every neural-net score
in this copy predates the clip of predictions to the training-label range, and hybrids are
