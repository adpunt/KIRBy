# f36: which label-smoothing methods the noise suite carries

Design note, 2026-09-30. Nothing is built. Every file:line below was read on
2026-09-30. Where `tests/experiments/noise/LABEL_SMOOTHING_EXPANSION.md`
(2026-09-25) disagrees with the code, the code is given and the difference is
listed in section 1c.

"Smoothing" here means any operation that moves a training label part or all of
the way toward a replacement value. Deleting a molecule and changing its weight
are not smoothing.

## 1. What exists

### 1a. The seven label-moving cleanups in n07

All in `tests/experiments/noise/n07_remediation.py`, `cleaned_training_set` (line 713).
How far a label moves is set by `smooth_by_scores`
(`src/kirby/utils/noise_estimation.py:2040`). The suspicion score comes from
n07's detector, `cross_val` (n07:483).

| cleanup | line | moves the label toward | how far | settings in code | classification |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `label_smooth` | 967 | mean label of 9 nearest training molecules, Euclidean (`min(10, n-1)` queried, the molecule itself dropped) | `strength` × suspicion score rescaled to 0..1 | `strength`, default 0.5 | yes: flagged share replaced by the neighbours' majority class |
| `knn_smooth` | 997 | the same 9-neighbour mean | flagged share, all the way | flag share | yes, majority class |
| `prox_smooth` | 1019 | mean label of the 10 molecules a 200-tree forest most often puts in the same leaf | flagged share, all the way | flag share | yes, majority class |
| `oof_smooth` | 1079 | out-of-fold LightGBM prediction, 5 scaffold-group-aware inner folds | as `label_smooth` | `strength`, default 0.5 | refused |
| `gp_smooth` | 1098 | Gaussian process posterior mean, 5 inner folds | as `label_smooth` | `strength`, default 0.5. Refused above 1,000 training molecules (n07:328) | refused |
| `js_shrink` | 916 | the same LightGBM prediction as `oof_smooth` | share = σ²ᵢ/(σ²ᵢ+τ²); τ² = variance of label minus prediction; σ²ᵢ = rescaled score × τ² × `strength` | `strength`, default 1.0 | refused |
| `quantile_clip` | 1174 | nearer edge of the 10th–90th percentile range of a quantile regression forest | only labels outside the range, onto the edge | none in n07 (edge at 1.0); n11 sweeps the share 0.1/0.3/0.5/0.7 | refused |

Flag share is swept at 0.05, 0.10, 0.20 (`FLAG_SHARES`, n07:233). Only
`remove`, `knn_smooth`, `prox_smooth`, and `label_smooth` on classification read it.

`smooth_by_scores` has four rules (noise_estimation.py:2060–2090):
`proportional` (the only one n07 uses), `threshold`, `soft_threshold` (logistic,
centre 0.5 and slope 5 hard-coded), `confidence` (raw score, not rescaled).
Only n11 sweeps all four, at share 0.1/0.3/0.5/0.7 (n11:174–176), toward three
targets: the detector's own, out-of-fold LightGBM, Gaussian process (n11:280).

### 1c. Where LABEL_SMOOTHING_EXPANSION.md is out of date

1. The conditional median is not thrown away. `QuantileForestEstimator` stores it
   as `predictions_` (noise_estimation.py:1552). n11 already smooths toward it:
   its target look-up (n11:311) reads `predictions_` for the `quantile_forest`
   detector. No n07 cleanup reads it: `correction_targets` keeps only the two
   edges (n07:651).
2. `prox_smooth` is no longer refused above 2,000 molecules.
   `PROX_SMOOTH_MAX_ROWS = None` (n07:337), owner's decision 2026-09-25.
3. n31's `bayes_shrink` is no longer an oracle. Its share has been estimated from
   the out-of-fold residuals since 2026-09-28 (n31:316, n31:974).
4. `label_smooth` and `knn_smooth` average 9 neighbours, not 10.
5. The flag share is no longer the injected fraction. It is swept at
   0.05/0.10/0.20 since 2026-09-28 (n07:224–233).

### 1d. The 13 options not built

Every one needs a target and a suspicion score as today; costs are code, not compute.

| option | what it computes | parameters | build cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| conditional median target | move toward the quantile forest's out-of-fold 0.5 quantile | none beyond the forest | small: add `'median'` to `correction_targets`, read `predictions_` |
| other quantile targets | the same at another quantile | the quantile, e.g. 0.25, 0.75 | small: the estimator takes `quantile_low/high`; needs a third quantile argument |
| near-duplicate pair mean | move each molecule in a close pair toward the pair's mean label; molecules in no close pair stay put | pair distance cut-off; n35 uses the 1st percentile of pairwise distances (n35:162) | medium: n35:214 `close_pairs` exists; memory is a molecules × molecules matrix, n35 caps at 12,000 rows (n35:170) |
| redescending shape | share rises with the residual, peaks, falls back to 0 for very large residuals | peak and zero point, in units of s = 1.4826 × median abs residual; Tukey's 4.685 (n33:186) is one zero point | small |
| uncertainty-gated shape | share divided by epistemic uncertainty, so labels move least where the target model is least sure | which model; a floor on the uncertainty | medium: only `hetero_gp` separates the two uncertainties in KIRBy; its out-of-fold latent variance is `latent_variance_` (noise_estimation.py:1976). Cubic in molecules |
| agreement-gated shape | share times agreement between two out-of-fold predictions | which two models; agreement as sign agreement or 1/(spread of the two) | small once two targets exist |
| preserve variance | after smoothing, rescale residuals about the mean back to the original spread | whole fold, or per scaffold group | small. Per group is undefined for 1-molecule groups |
| preserve group mean | subtract each group's mean change, so no group's mean label moves | the grouping | small |
| preserve rank | map smoothed values back onto the original sorted labels | none | small |
| fixed-point passes | smooth, refit the target on the smoothed labels, repeat until labels stop moving | tolerance; max passes | medium: each pass refits the target (5 inner fits) |
| annealed passes | as above, share falls each pass | starting share; decay; passes | medium, same refit cost |
| residual scale | smooth residuals from a base model, add the prediction back | which base model | small |
| rank scale | smooth the ranks, map back through the fold's label distribution | none | small |

Not listed and not built, each for a stated reason: count-scaled share (needs
repeat measurements, none of the three datasets has them); log scale (undefined
on logd, which has negative labels).

## 3. Proposed grid (owner chooses)

A **task** is dataset × model input × model × noise condition. Draws and folds
are repeats inside a task.

### Option (a): each method at the settings the suite already uses, one at a time. 72 settings.

Existing, 54: label_smooth, oof_smooth, gp_smooth at strength 0.25/0.5/1.0 (9);
knn_smooth, prox_smooth at flag share 0.05/0.10/0.20 (6); js_shrink at 0.5/1/2 (3);
quantile_clip at share 0.1/0.3/0.5/0.7/1.0 (5); the other three rules
(`threshold`, `soft_threshold`, `confidence`) toward out-of-fold LightGBM at 0.5
(3); n27 fixed share 0.25/0.5/1.0 (3); n30 winsorise c = 1.0/1.25/1.5 (3); n30's
three lifts (3); n31 rewrite and bayes_shrink at 3 flag shares (6); n32's two
shrinks at 3 flag shares (6); n33 Huber teacher at 0.5 (1); n34's three (3);
n41 at q = 0.05/0.1/0.2 (3).

New, 18: median target at 0.25/0.5/1.0 (3); quantiles 0.25, 0.75 at 0.5 (2);
pair mean at 0.5, 1.0 (2); redescending, zero point 3s and 4.685s (2);
uncertainty-gated (1); agreement-gated (1); three preservations on oof_smooth at
0.5 (3); fixed-point and annealed (2); residual and rank scale (2). These
parameter values are my proposal, not taken from any file.

### Option (b): target × how-far crossed, the rest one at a time. 125 settings.

Targets, 6: 9-neighbour mean, forest-proximity mean, out-of-fold LightGBM,
Gaussian process, conditional median, near-duplicate pair mean.

How far, 15: proportional 0.25/0.5/1.0; fixed share 0.25/0.5/1.0; flagged-only
all the way at 0.05/0.10/0.20; variance ratio (js_shrink) at 0.5/1/2; dead zone
(winsorise) at c = 1.0/1.5; redescending at 4.685s.

6 × 15 = 90, plus 35 one at a time: quantile_clip 5, lifts 3, n32 shrinks 6,
n34 3, Huber teacher 1, other rules 3, n41 3, other quantiles 2,
uncertainty-gated 1, agreement-gated 1, preservations 3, passes 2, scales 2.

## 5. What was built, 2026-10-01

Owner's answers 2026-09-30/10-01: every combination; today's neighbours only
(n42 already sweeps the neighbour choice for these computations,
`n42_similarity_noise_sweep.py:41-46`); herg on `uniform`; framework-local.

Files: `f36_label_smoothing_portfolio.py` (the run), `f36_analyse.py` (the
report), `_smoke_f36.py` (synthetic check). `noise_helpers.run_all` learned the
id `f36` so it loads data and the precompute the way every noise experiment does.

### The combinations

new y = y + a × (v − y), crossed over:

- v, 12: 9-neighbour mean; forest-proximity 10-neighbour mean; out-of-fold
  LightGBM; the same with a Huber loss; Gaussian process; quantile forest 0.25,
  0.5, 0.75; near-duplicate pair mean; hetero_gp, ngboost, heteroscedastic_nn
  out-of-fold predictions. Two more read from n23 are the same computation as
  one of these and are not run twice (`VALUES_NOT_RUN` in the code).
- a, 31 (`RULES` in the code).
- preserve, 4: none, variance, group mean, rank.
- scale, 3: raw, residual, rank.
- passes, 3: once, repeat to a fixed point, annealed.

12 × 31 × 4 × 3 × 3 = 13,392. Of these, 2,232 are not run: residual scale with
preserve none or group mean gives the same labels as raw scale, by algebra, and
the smoke checks it on 186 rule × preserve × passes combinations (largest
difference 1.4e-15 label units). 11,160 are run per regression unit.

herg: 2 neighbour majorities × 3 flag shares × passes once or fixed = 12.

### Parameter values that are mine, not from any file

Redescending share 4u(1−u) with u = |y − v| / (c·s), zero above u = 1, c = 3
and 4.685. Uncertainty gate share 0.5 × floor / uncertainty, uncertainty =
hetero_gp's out-of-fold latent variance on D, floor its 10th percentile.
Agreement gate share 0.5 × exp(−|LightGBM − 9-neighbour mean| / s). Fixed
point: stop when no label moves more than 1e-3 × the label SD, at most 5
passes. Annealed: 3 passes, move halved each pass.

### Size

A unit is (condition, level, draw, fold, model, model input). Every level of
each condition is run (`dataset_policy.noise_levels`), 3 draws (1 at level 0
on regression), 5 folds, rf and svm, 3 model inputs each.

| dataset | units per passes schedule | combinations per unit | D' fits |
|---|---|---|---|
| logd | 1,620 | 3,720 per schedule | 18,079,200 |
| flab_expression_koenig | 1,620 | 3,720 per schedule | 18,079,200 |
| herg | 540 | 6 per schedule | 6,480 |

Under passes fixed and anneal, every pass after the first refits v on the moved
labels. For the 9 model-fitted v's that is up to 4 (fixed) or 2 (anneal) more
fits of 5 inner folds each, per combination, per unit.
