POSSIBLE EXPLANATIONS:
1. Gauche uses GP-based uncertainty that may be dominated by
   kernel hyperparameters rather than noise estimation
2. The model may be underfitting and capturing only epistemic
   uncertainty from limited training data
3. Hyperparameters may not be re-optimized for noisy conditions
